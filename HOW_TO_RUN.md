# 📖 TuneForge Quick Guide

A simple guide on how to run TuneForge for yourself or share it with anyone on **iPhone, iPad, or Android**.

---

## 🚗 Option 1: For Yourself (Car USB Pendrive)

When you want to download songs directly to your computer for your car audio system:

1. Double-click [**`start.bat`**](start.bat).
2. TuneForge will automatically open in your browser at `http://127.0.0.1:8000`.
3. Paste any link from:
   - **YouTube** (Single video or full playlist)
   - **YouTube Music** (Album or playlist)
   - **Spotify** (Track, album, or playlist)
   - **Apple Music** (Song, album, or playlist)
4. Click **Start Download**.
5. When finished (all tracks turn 🟢), click **"Open Folder"** and copy the MP3 files to your USB pendrive!

---

## 📱 Option 2: Share with a Friend (iPhone / iPad / Android)

When your friend wants to download songs directly onto their phone/tablet without needing a PC:

1. Double-click [**`share_tunnel.bat`**](share_tunnel.bat).
2. A terminal window will open and display a box with a link ending in `.trycloudflare.com`:
   ```text
   +-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
   |  Your quick Tunnel has been created! Visit it at:                             |
   |  https://xxxx-xxxx-xxxx.trycloudflare.com                                     |
   +-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
   ```
3. **Copy that link and send it to your friend** on WhatsApp, Telegram, or iMessage.
4. **Keep the terminal window open on your PC** while your friend uses the app.

---

## 📲 What Your Friend Does on Mobile:

1. Opens your link in **Safari (iPhone/iPad)** or **Chrome (Android)**.
2. Pastes any playlist or song URL and taps **Download**.
3. Once the queue finishes:
   - **Whole Playlist:** They tap **`Save All (ZIP)`** to download all songs in 1 zip file directly to their phone's **Files / Downloads** app!
   - **Single Song:** They tap the **Download arrow icon** next to any song to save that individual MP3.
